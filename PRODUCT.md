# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS as a prototype, preview via GitHub Pages (RITTHAPPENS/craig). Production build later in WordPress/Elementor.

## Users

Private homeowners and property owners in Thüringen (Bad Berka, Erfurt, Weimar, Jena and surroundings) who are planning an outdoor area, a renovation or a building project, or who suspect structural damage. They arrive via Google or Google Ads, usually on mobile, and check within seconds whether this company is right for their project and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

Website for Bauservice Craig, an owner-run construction company from Bad Berka. Goal: qualified enquiries via Google, a clear presentation of the three areas of expertise, and trust built through real projects and people.

## Positioning

Three areas of expertise from one hand: construction company (execution), site management and construction supervision, building surveys and damage assessment. Marvin Craig is a DEKRA-certified expert in building damage assessment, so the same person who builds also knows how to spot damage. Owner-run (Marvin: technical side, Carissa: commercial side and brand).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: landscaping and outdoor areas (paving, terraces, earthworks and excavator work, retaining and concrete work), pool construction, interior fit-out, renovation and modernisation, tiling and flooring, bathroom renovation, site management and construction supervision, building surveyor and building surveys.
- SEO: the homepage targets "bauunternehmen thüringen", "baufirma thüringen" and "bauunternehmen bad berka". Service pages are linked, not duplicated.
- 10 pages at launch, Thüringen-wide; city landing pages come later.
- Contact: info@bauservice-craig.de, 0160 7625394, Zum Erfurter Tal 6, 99438 Bad Berka.

## Brand Commitments

- Slogan on the site banner: "Ihre Vision, unsere Mission". Values: quality, competence, reliability.
- Logo: houses/roofs mark, black/grey (#737373). The style guide is still open.
- The client wants to stand out deliberately from classic tradesperson websites: high-end, trustworthy, personal.
- Copy: no dashes as stylistic devices, no filler text.

## Product Principles

1. Real projects and real people instead of stock images.
2. Make the three areas of expertise immediately understandable.
3. One clear path to an enquiry, by phone or form.
4. Regional proximity (Thüringen, Bad Berka) shown visibly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA, mobile first, fast loading (Core Web Vitals).
